In short

Product designer at TCS Interactive since 2022, working on pfizer.com and with teams at Johnson & Johnson.

Pretty screens are easy. I back mine with research I run myself: interviews, workflow mapping and testing with scientists and the public. Everything meets WCAG 2.1 AA, and I prototype in code every day, because a static mock never told anyone the truth.

Now finishing an MA in Design Management at LCC, UAL, and looking for product design roles with a hard problem at the centre, in any sector.
