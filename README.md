# Sisters Apart

A 3D city-discovery game about two sisters. Emma (25, photographer) is on a
residency in **New York**; Sophie (23, musician) is interning in **London**.
They haven't seen each other in six months, and they've both decided to fix
that without telling each other.

You live their daily lives: eating, working, making friends, getting lost.
Along the way you notice clues that your sister isn't where she says she is.
You pin them to a mystery board, work out where she is, chase a trail of
paper lanterns across the city, and meet her on a bridge at sunset.

It runs in the browser. It's built with [three.js](https://threejs.org) and Vite,
and every model, texture and sound is generated by code. There are no asset files.

## Running it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # http://localhost:5173
npm run build      # static build in dist/ (open with `npm run preview`)
npm run build:artifact   # one self-contained HTML page in dist-artifact/
```

`build:artifact` inlines the game's code and CSS into a single page. That page
loads three.js from jsdelivr through an import map, so you can host it anywhere
that serves one HTML file.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **WASD** / arrows | Walk (hold **Shift** to run) |
| Drag mouse / wheel | Orbit and zoom the camera |
| **E** | Talk, enter places, pick options |
| **Tab** / **P** | Phone: messages, tasks, transit, journal, people, local picks, settings |
| **B** | Mystery board (detective mode) |
| **M** | District map |
| **C** | Camera. Drag or WASD to aim, wheel or Q/Z to zoom, click or Space to shoot |
| **J** | Journal |
| **1–5** | Pick dialogue/menu options, play notes in music sessions |

Touch devices get an on-screen joystick and buttons.

## What's in it

**Two cities with five districts each.**

- New York: Brooklyn (Williamsburg and the Brooklyn Bridge), Midtown (Times Square
  and the Empire State), the Village (Washington Square Arch), Central Park
  (Bethesda Terrace, the Lake, Bow Bridge) and the Upper West Side (the natural
  history museum).
- London: King's Cross (St Pancras), South Bank (the Eye, Waterloo Bridge, Big Ben
  across the river), Notting Hill (pastel terraces, Portobello), Camden (the Lock
  and the market) and Bloomsbury (the British Museum).

**Living streets.** Traffic drives on the correct side of the road for each city
(yellow cabs in New York; black cabs and red double-deckers in London). Traffic
stops and honks if you stand in the road. Pedestrians walk the blocks.

**Time and weather.** The sun moves across the sky and lighting changes from
dawn to golden hour to night. Windows light up after dark. It rains in London
far more than in New York, and streets look wet when it does. New York has
autumn leaves falling.

**Daily-life loop.**
- *Eat*: every café, cart and market has a menu. Each sister has favourite kinds
  of food that give extra mood.
- *Work*: Emma takes photo assignments that you shoot with the in-game camera.
  Framing and light are scored. Sophie does ear-training sessions and practice,
  and later plays gigs (a call-and-response music minigame).
- *Hang out*: galleries, museums, jazz clubs, bookshops, parks.
- *Explore*: hidden gems (with hints in the Local Picks app), photo spots and
  a new-neighbourhood memory for each district.
- *Needs*: energy, hunger, mood and money. Sleeping at home ends the day with an
  evening reflection.

**People.** Ten characters per city, each with a daily schedule, dialogue that
changes by story phase, humour and cultural observations, lines unlocked by
friendship, and a secret connection to your sister. Friendships unlock rewards:
a bike-share key for sprint speed, roof access that adds a photo spot, a stage
for gigs, and a dinner reservation with a possible love interest.

**Investigation.** Clues come from conversations, overheard talk in cafés,
details in your own photographs, and phone messages. They go on the Mystery
Board under four deductions:

1. Where is she, really?
2. Where is she staying?
3. What does her day look like?
4. Where will she be waiting?

You solve a deduction by choosing the answer your clues support. Wrong answers
get a funny rebuttal and no penalty.

**Six phases.** Arrival (tutorial), City Integration, Investigation Intensifies,
Pursuit (a real-time lantern trail across three districts), Reunion (a sunset
scene on the bridge), and an Epilogue where you play as your sister.

**Replay.**
- Emma's New York story is the first playthrough.
- Sophie's London story is the New Game+, set the following spring when Emma
  flies over to return the favour.
- The **true ending** needs seven close friends (3♥), every district visited
  and six good photographs.
- **Speed Run** shows a timer and records your best time.
- **Photo Perfect** (Emma only) gives you 24 exposures in total.

**Audio.** Procedural WebAudio: a jazz trio for New York (walking bass,
brushes, swung comping) and chamber pop for London (pizzicato arpeggios and
pads). Ambience covers traffic, rain, café chatter and subway rumble. The
sisters' childhood song, "Paper Lanterns", comes back at key moments.

Progress autosaves to `localStorage` when you sleep, when you travel, and
every minute. Settings (graphics quality, sound, labels) are in the phone.

## How the brief maps onto a browser game

Some parts of the original design brief can't be done in a browser game with
no assets, so they were adapted:

- **Photorealism and ray tracing** became a stylised low-poly look using
  real-time PBR, shadows, bloom and a sky environment map. Building windows
  are generated in a shader from world position, so every building shares one
  material and districts stay light enough for mid-range hardware.
- **Voice acting** became written dialogue with stage directions, plus
  procedural music and sound.
- **Real landmarks** are simplified, recognisable stand-ins. Businesses and
  characters are fictional.

## Project layout

```
src/
  main.js            entry point
  audio.js           procedural music, ambience and SFX
  style.css          all UI styling
  data/              story content: nyc.js, london.js, common.js
  world/             city generator, landmarks, sky/weather, traffic, characters
  game/              game loop, player controller, story rules, save state
  ui/                HUD/dialogue/menus/minigame (ui.js) and phone/board/map (phone.js)
```

To add content, edit the files in `src/data/`. Districts are 5×5 grids of
blocks (`grid` strings), and points of interest are placed by block and side
or by coordinates. NPCs, clues, deductions, messages, the pursuit and the
endings are all plain data.
